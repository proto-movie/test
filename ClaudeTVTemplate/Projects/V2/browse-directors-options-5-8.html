Directors in Browse

Four more ways to bring Films & Series, Collections and Directors together in one Browse, each based on a common TV app pattern: a side rail for the whole nav, browse rows, large section cards and a title dropdown. Pick one to open the prototype. In every option, selecting a director opens their page with their films on MUBI (UK catalogue).

Options 1–4 Options 5–8
Option 5

Side rail

The top nav moves into a rail down the left of every page: Now Showing, Films & Series, Directors, Collections, Language, Watchlist and Search, with Account & Settings at the bottom. It sits as icons and opens out with labels when you press Left at the edge of a page or Back.

  • Films & Series, Directors, Collections and Language are top-level destinations
  • Up and Down move along the rail, Enter opens a page, Right goes back to where you were
  • Back on the rail returns to Now Showing

Based on: Android TV / Google TV navigation drawer, the Netflix TV menu and the Apple TV app sidebar (tvOS 17.2).

Option 6

Browse rows

Browse opens on a page of rows: genres, collection groups and directors. Pick a genre or group to open it with its pills; pick a director to go straight to their page. The remote's Back returns to the rows.

  • Shows a taste of all three at once
  • Directors are one press from Browse

Based on: Netflix and Android TV browse rows (Leanback BrowseFragment).

Open prototype →
Option 7

Section cards

Browse opens on a row of large cards, the size of Now Showing's double tiles: Films & Series, Collections, Directors and Language. Selecting a card opens that section with its pills; the remote's Back returns to the cards.

  • Clearest choice of the sections
  • Language lists the original languages of the films on MUBI UK as pills, from English and French to Korean and Persian
  • Room for artwork that sets the tone of each

Based on: Apple TV and Disney+ hub tiles, Google TV immersive cards.

Open prototype →
Option 8

Title dropdown

The page title is a dropdown. Press Up to the title, then Enter, and pick Films & Series, Collections or Directors; the pills and grid switch to that section.

  • Keeps the current Browse layout almost unchanged
  • The title always says where you are

Based on: The Netflix TV category picker and Google TV tabs.
