MUBI TV · Browse prototype

Directors in Browse

Four ways to bring the website's director browsing to the TV app: as a section of Browse alongside Films & Series and Collections, as a tab among the genres, as its own page in the top nav, or as a group of pills in a single Browse row that also holds the genres and collections. Pick one to open the prototype. In every option, selecting a director opens their page with a back arrow and their films on MUBI (UK catalogue).

Option 1

Browse sections

Browse holds three sections: Films & Series, Collections and Directors. All three sit in the title row in a fixed order, with the section you're in shown in white, and underlined while the row has focus. Move Left or Right along the row and the page underneath switches to that section.

  • Collections moves out of the top nav and into Browse
  • The top nav stays short: Now Showing, Browse, Watchlist
  • Directors filters: All, Emerging, Women, Classic Auteurs, Award Winning

Option 2

Directors tab in Browse

Browse stays as it is, with a Directors tab added to the genre row, right after Recently Added. Selecting it swaps the film grid for director tiles.

  • Smallest change to the current navigation
  • Directors sorted by number of films on MUBI
Open prototype →
Option 3

Directors in the top nav

Watchlist leaves the top nav and Directors takes its place. Directors gets its own page that follows the Browse and Collections template.

  • Directors is one press away from anywhere
  • Same filters as option 1
Open prototype →
Option 4

One Browse, grouped pills

A single Browse page with one pill row, grouped: genres (including Series), then collections, then directors. The page title follows the group you're in (Films & Series, Collections or Directors), and each pill shows the right kind of tile.

  • Collections moves out of the top nav and into Browse
  • Collection pills: Spotlight, Award Winners, Director Retrospectives, Decades, Around the World
  • Director pills: All Directors, Women Directors, Emerging Directors, Classic Auteurs, Award Winning
Open prototype →

Prototypes are built for a 1920×1080 TV screen and driven with the arrow keys, Enter and Esc / Backspace for back.
